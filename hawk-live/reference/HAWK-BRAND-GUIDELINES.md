# HAWK ERP Brand & UI Guidelines

Status: approved by the user as the shared design baseline for HAWK modules on 11 November 2026.

## References

- [HAWK ERP NV-01](https://preview.hawk.jaens.id/#/nv/1) is the visual reference for the application shell and dashboard.
- [Commercial OS prototype](commercial/preview.html) shows the approved direction applied to a separate portal.

## Application shell

- Use the H mark with the HAWK ERP wordmark and JAENS ENTERPRISES descriptor. Use the official asset when available. Keep logo proportions and placement consistent across portals.
- Use a charcoal sidebar and top bar, warm ivory surfaces, and restrained gold gradients for the brand mark, primary controls, and active navigation. Use the system sans-serif hierarchy seen in NV-01.
- Provide a responsive shell with readable content, keyboard-visible focus, clear active navigation, and access to portal switching on mobile.
- The clickable HAWK ERP logo is the shared entry point for portal selection. Keep Finance & ERP, Operational Therapist, and Commercial OS as separate destinations with their own navigation. Commercial is the third entry. Use verified deployment routes when wiring the shared selector.

## Dashboards

- Follow the NV-01 hierarchy of page context, title, role summary, KPI cards, and detail panels. Use the black-to-gold treatment for prominent summaries and warm ivory cards for detail.
- Use green, violet, blue, and gold KPI gradients where they support scanability. For charts and statuses, pair semantic color with labels, values, or line patterns. Include units and accessible text or tables for key values.
- Mark demos and simulated AI visibly. Commercial references shared Finance 7P Budgeting, Purchasing/MRF, Inventory, and approval workflows through the existing ERP integration points.
